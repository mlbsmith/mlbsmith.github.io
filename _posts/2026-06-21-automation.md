title: On Automation, and Fatherhood
---

I have a lot of thoughts on AI, but we'll get to that another day.

Breathless automation can sometimes cause us to lose the plot a bit.

Optimization is hard to compartmentalize.
Automation is closer to a hammer than a scalpel.

I am thankful for the embodied humanness of a toddler.
Absolutely nothing she does is efficient.

At the end of the day, automation is about control.
It's about breaking down a task into pieces that can run unsupervised, whether that is by a machine, a computer, or another human.

Nothing will remind you of your utter lack of control like trying to plan a day with a 1 year old.

Happy Father's Day.
